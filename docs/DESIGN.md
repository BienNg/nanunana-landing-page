## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, academic yet empathetic spirit tailored for Vietnamese students and young professionals seeking higher education, vocational training (Ausbildung), and career opportunities in Germany. 

### Brand Personality & Core Audience
- **Target Audience:** Vietnamese high schoolers, university graduates, and aspiring professionals aged 16–30, alongside pragmatic parents evaluating high-stakes educational investments abroad.
- **Personality Pillars:** Trustworthy, encouraging, meticulous, and globally aspirational. It dismantles the intimidation of learning German and navigating European bureaucracy.
- **Tone & Mood:** Bright, modern, and transparent. It blends European academic rigour with an approachable, energetic community ethos.

### Visual Style Movement
The design system operates at the intersection of **Contemporary European Editorial** and **Crisp EdTech Minimalism**:
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Clean off-white and cool mist backdrops replace sterile pure whites, reducing cognitive fatigue during dense research.
- **Layered Precision:** Restrained structural borders, subtle elevation steps, and distinct course badges build clear visual signposts.
- **Warm Conversion Cues:** Strategic amber and coral accents inject warmth into high-priority interactions, breaking up deep slate and cyan structures to drive engagement without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep slates with vivid German-education teal and warm call-to-action amber:

- **Primary Teal (`#008DA5` / `#00A3BF`):** The primary identity color. Communicates focus, international progress, and modern academic guidance. Used for active navigation, key icons, primary buttons, and hero highlights.
- **Secondary Slate/Navy (`#0F172A`):** Anchors headlines, primary typography, dark footer blocks, and structural dividers to provide high-contrast legibility.
- **Tertiary & Conversion Amber/Coral (`#F59E0B` / `#EE872A`):** Reserved for high-intent conversion actions (e.g., "Đăng Ký Tư Vấn Ngay", scholarship countdowns, urgent intake alerts).
- **Neutral System (`#64748B`, `#E2E8F0`, `#F8FAFC`):** Slate grays provide accessible body copy and soft card perimeters on top of a soft mist background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Pedagogical CEFR Badges:** Unique pastel tints and high-contrast foreground inks designate language tiers (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and Giao Tiếp), enabling users to scan course tracks at a glance.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, open counters, and high legibility across accented Vietnamese diacritics and German loanwords.

- **Weight Pairing:** Bold and Extra-Bold (700/800) weights command headlines with authority, while Medium and Regular (500/400) deliver sustained readability in curriculum descriptions and consular guides.
- **Diacritic Protection:** Line-height values preserve clearance for Vietnamese tone markers (hỏi, ngã, nặng) and German umlauts (ä, ö, ü), preventing vertical collision across responsive wrapping.
- **Micro-Copy & CEFR Metrics:** Course codes, schedule tags, and trust indicators use uppercase label tracking (`+0.04em`) to establish crisp metadata blocks.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with defined boundary maximums (`max-width: 1240px`) to prevent sprawling content on ultra-wide viewports:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins. Used for multi-column course comparisons, visa consultation funnels, and faculty profiles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, reflowing complex split layouts into 4-column balanced modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins. Stacks cards vertically while sticky bottom-anchored CTA trays maintain reachability.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section headers are cushioned with `space-xl` spacing, while related form fields and card internals maintain a compact `space-sm` to `space-md` cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs calibrated ambient shadows coupled with soft surface outlines:

- **Surface Tinting:** Card surfaces are rendered in crisp `#FFFFFF` placed over the foundation `#F8FAFC` canvas, generating natural separation without heavy borders.
- **Restrained Outlines:** A `1px` border using `#E2E8F0` defines all interactive modules, maintaining structure in bright ambient conditions.
- **Tier 1 (Flat Cards / Passive Containers):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Cards / Consultation Modules):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Menus / Mobile Lead Forms / Sticky CTAs):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(0, 141, 165, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Subtle teal diffusion provides brand cohesion.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Rounded (`2`) geometry**:

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** Standardized to `0.5rem` (8px) for a modern, tactile feel.
- **Cards, Modals, and Media Wrappers:** Standardized to `1rem` (16px, `rounded-lg`) to soften structured data tables, program overviews, and student testimonials.
- **Badges and Pills:** Fully curved (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for CEFR tags, scholarship availability markers, and live intake statuses.
- **Hero & Feature Media:** Framed using `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) with subtle internal clipping masks for community and classroom imagery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Lead Capture / Register):** Solid `#EE872A` with `#FFFFFF` text. On hover: shifts to `#D97706` with a slight 1px vertical lift and soft glow. Used for primary conversion triggers ("Đăng Ký Tư Vấn", "Nhận Lộ Trình").
- **Secondary Action (Curriculum Explorer):** Solid `#008DA5` with `#FFFFFF` text. On hover: `#00A3BF`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` border in `#008DA5` and teal typography.
- **Padding:** Vertical `0.75rem`, Horizontal `1.5rem` for standard buttons; `label-lg` typography.

### Course Level Badges & Chips
- **Level Identifiers (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, Giao Tiếp):** Pill-shaped indicators using the designated pastel background and dark text tokens (e.g., `#E0F2FE` with `#0369A1` for A1; `#FFEDD5` with `#C2410C` for B2).
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive: `#F1F5F9` surface with `#475569` text. Active: `#008DA5` surface with `#FFFFFF` text and checkmark indicator.

### Cards
- **Program & Pathway Cards:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` border `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg`, `space-lg` inner padding. Header displays the course level badge and duration, body features curriculum milestones with teal checkmarks, footer contains the action button and pricing/intake dates.
- **Mentor & Faculty Cards:** Circular avatar framed with a subtle teal border, accompanied by bilingual certification badges (e.g., "Cử nhân Magdeburg", "Goethe C1/C2").

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Form Controls:** Minimum touch height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px` solid `#CBD5E1`, rounded `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#008DA5` with a 3px outer ring tint (`rgba(0, 141, 165, 0.15)`).
- **Floating Labels:** Crisp `12px` label positioning with dark slate tone `#0F172A`.

### Trust Signals & Conversion Points
- **Sticky Lead Anchor (Mobile):** Anchored bottom panel with direct action buttons: "Zalo Chat", "Gọi Hotline", and "Đăng Ký".
- **Social Proof Badges:** Micro-components displaying star ratings, Goethe-Zertifikat pass rates (e.g., "98% Đỗ B1 Lần Đầu"), and verified German university admission tallies.
- **Guaranteed Pathway Badges:** Shield-shaped icon indicators paired with `#10B981` (Emerald) supporting texts.
